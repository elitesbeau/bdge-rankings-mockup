THE TAKE
AI v4.2 · 78% confidence · The BDGE Desk

Workhorse RB1 with no concrete handcuff threat — still the alpha. Ceiling caps the room and the floor is among the highest at the position.

Floor-driven CEO who hits ceiling more reliably than any back since Faulk. Take him at the 1.01 and don't think twice. BDGE Desk · v4.2

The 49ers funnel every meaningful touch through McCaffrey when he's available, and the 2026 cap structure says he's available all year. Backup Jordan Mason has flashed spike weeks but no path to a 3-down role.

Age 29 is the historical RB cliff. Comps say the cliff is real, but the 24-month load profile and the 2024 receiving-game reset suggest a softer landing than the position average. The bet: durability holds. The Niners stay top-5 in scripted touch volume.
