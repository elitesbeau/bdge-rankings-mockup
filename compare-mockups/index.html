Tug-of-War Meter

One horizontal needle bar across the top. Each stat row contributes a slice. Sliders shift the pointer live. The bar IS the decision.

Dock v2 · Plinko

Plinko Lanes

Five horizontal channels, one per stat. Pucks physically slide toward whichever player wins. Aggregate puck at the bottom delivers the verdict.

Dock v2 · Pip Grid

Pip Grid

Minimalist scoreboard. Each row's pips light up by margin; weights dim or boost rows. Total row = rounds won.

→ Cleanest, glanceable

Direction v1 — early ideas (rejected). Five takeover-style approaches I built first. Kept here for reference; not the path forward.

VERDICT
Mockup 1 · Recommendation-first

The Verdict

A giant pick with a confidence %, four hero stats as duel bars, and a one-sentence reason. Decide in five seconds, override if you disagree.

→ Removes decision fatigue
TALE / TAPE
Mockup 2 · Sports broadcast

Tale of the Tape

UFC pre-fight card. Two portraits face off, composite scores in giant numerals, five "rounds" each with a checkmark winner, final scorecard.

→ Familiar mental model
RADAR
Mockup 3 · Visual gestalt

Radar Overlay

Two overlapping spider charts on six axes. Bigger blob wins. Hover an axis to drill into the underlying stat. Pure pattern recognition.

→ One image = decision
TRADEOFF
Mockup 4 · Situational decision

Tradeoff Lens

Floor-vs-Ceiling scatter with outcome clouds. Four situational prompts ("Need safety?" "Need upside?") highlight the contextual winner.

→ Frames context, not numbers
2:00
Mockup 5 · Time pressure

The Clock

Live 2:00 countdown ticking at top. Five battle cards with glowing winner sides. Two huge "DRAFT" buttons. Embraces the clock as the design driver.

→ Theatrical, keeps you moving
