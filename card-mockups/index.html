Player Card Variations

3 ideas to fill the empty space

Today's player cards feel under-filled — the middle column is a name + a small position pill, and the rest of the height is dead space. Three different ways to add useful info without making the card feel cluttered.

DATA STRIP
Variation 1

Data Strip

Smallest change. Add a single horizontal strip of compact stats below the name: PROJ · PPG · BYE · AGE. Subtle, dense, no new layout.
TIER POSTER
Variation 2

Tier Poster

Bigger PFP with team-tinted background, tier ribbon (ELITE / ALL-PRO / STARTER) on top-left, prominent name. Card reads as a poster.
STAT PILLS
Variation 3

Stat Pills

Keep the existing card; add a row of colored stat chips at the bottom: PROJ 336 · FLR 251 · CEIL 398 · VBD +62. Scan-friendly.
