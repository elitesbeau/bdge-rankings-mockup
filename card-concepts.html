The 49ers' workhorse enters his ninth year as the only true CEO at his position. Volume isn't a question. Durability is.
BY The BDGE Desk UPDATED 2 minutes ago RANK 01 / 300
Story Numbers Projection Vegas Impact Field Comps Community

Workhorse RB1 with no concrete handcuff threat — still the alpha of the 49ers' offense. Ceiling caps the room and the floor is among the highest at the position. Volume risk attached to durability profile, but the 24-month injury record now reads cleaner than it has at any point since his rookie deal.

Backup Jordan Mason has flashed spike weeks but no path to a 3-down role. Kyle Shanahan funnels every meaningful touch through McCaffrey when he's available, and the 2026 cap structure says he's available all year.

"Floor-driven CEO who hits ceiling more reliably than any back since Faulk." — BDGE Desk · Outlook v4.2 · 78% confidence

The Numbers

Projection model returns 348.2 PPR points at the median, with a 90th-percentile ceiling of 412 and a 10th-percentile floor of 268. VBD versus replacement back is +62.4, the highest at the position by a 14-point margin. Consensus ADP sits at 2.1 overall, which is the cheapest he has been since 2022.

The Risk

Age 29 is the historical RB cliff. Comps say the cliff is real, but the 24-month load profile and the 2024 receiving-game reset suggest a softer landing than the position average. The bet is on durability holding for one more year — and on the 49ers' offense staying near the league's top five in scripted touch volume.
